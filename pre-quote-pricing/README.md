# Pre-quote pricing — Kevin-call prep

Sibling workstream to the product-licensing intake. Where the intake models the
part-number/catalog lifecycle, this models the pricing changes that happen
**before a quote goes out** (discounts, exceptions, new-product pricing, promos,
region/currency, bundles, custom parts).

**The reframe driving this:** the ticket log doesn't record these requests
reliably, and the one person who knows is out. That's not a blocker — it's the
headline finding. The requirements live with the people who do the work and in
the shadow systems (email, Teams, spreadsheets), so we gather from there and aim
it all at the scoping call as a straw man to validate, not a blank page.

## What's here

| File | What it is | Use it to |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| `request-type-catalog.html` | Straw-man catalog: six request types, their fields, routing, and where each breaks today. Built from first principles + the one worked example. | Open in a browser (or print). The centerpiece the call reacts to. |
| `requirements-questionnaire.md` | One-page questionnaire for pre-quote practitioners (Deal Desk, Pricing Ops, field). | Send to 4–5 people; it validates the catalog and asks them to forward real threads. |
| `note-to-michelle.md` | Short draft asking for the two tear-sheet docs. | Fill the brackets and send. |
